FAQ

Common questions.

Who can attend?

Anyone interested in multimodal safety, robustness, privacy, and deployed AI systems is welcome.

Is the workshop hybrid?

We aim to support both in-person and remote participation where possible.

What kinds of submissions are accepted?

We welcome position papers, short papers, and early-stage research that address open challenges in real-world multimodal safety.

Do I need to be a domain expert?

No. We encourage interdisciplinary participation from ML, HCI, systems, policy, design, and adjacent fields.
